R26 measures fixed textbook computation trees, ordered by plausible code shape.
No match-count ranking, fitted coefficient, tolerance, threshold or row selector.

fingerprint.py is the one new reusable analysis tool. It uses the existing
Excel scalar service and existing registered application prediction route.
Successful replies retain exact requested operands, actual Value2 readbacks,
typed answers and worker provenance in small gzip files. Refused requests and
their raw replies are retained as failures, without measured input windows.
Ideal mathematical leaf bounds come from the installed official MPFR API;
they are not private Excel instructions or observations.

The map covers every problem-list entry, plus explicit elementary controls.
Two consecutive-double windows provide output spectra. Affine detrending,
a Hann window and a radix2 DFT retain Nyquist and ordinary local peaks.
Peaks, harmonics and aliases do not identify the number of hidden stores.
Even a complete matching tree identifies stores only within its declared
family: inserting an exact, redundant store need not change any output.
No map entry is credited with an identified store count from spectra alone.

GEOMEAN's product is separated from its unknown root by actual independent
GEOMEAN(candidateProduct,1,1) readings. Exact-power redistributions test the
product-before-root hypothesis. The square tie with power-of-two third factors
separates strict53 from storedPC64. Non-power third factors separate a store
after each multiply from a residentPC64 accumulator with one final store.
The independent checker imports no product/search model. It recomputes exact
products, checks every raw reading, and audits every changed replay output.
Every observed loss is classified as the unresolved root at its independently
measured stored product. A private resident extended-root consumer is outside
this binary64-root-boundary certificate. Overflow/underflow remains open.

The two live replay scopes, canonical GEOMEAN bank, whole frozen corpus and
new R24 coverage are kept separate. Duplicate root controls are not unique
new calls. No cached workbook result or model prediction scores as calculated.
R24 keeps original workbook bytes, original answers, formula rewrites and
effective operand echoes. Intended decimal neighbors need not survive Excel
parsing. The application comparison uses the actual captured workbook.

BINOM/POISSON programs use fixed direct polynomials, finite products/sums,
plain recurrences, then explicitly conditional public-LN/EXP/POWER leaves.
Mathematical LOG1P candidates distinguish an exact complement from a prior
binary64 subtraction; no hypothetical private leaf counts as an observation.
Every profile and first contradiction is retained, including all losses.
GEOMEAN unit-factor root programs vary arity2..32 with all products exact.
The native x87 root grammar exclusions are conditional on this AMD EPYC CPU,
its measured single-instruction leaves and the declared grammar only.

prove global monotonicity or a hidden threshold expression.
