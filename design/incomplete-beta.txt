Incomplete beta (BETA.DIST, and the BINOM.DIST CDF that reads it): design note
(round 43, Claude Opus, dna-firehorse)

Sources: Excel answers (question service, Excel 16.0.20527), mpmath for the
true values, the TOMS 708 bratio already in calc-func (cleared). No tier 3/4
implementation was read.

Why here: BINOM.DIST(k, n, p, TRUE) for 0 < k < n is the regularized beta at
q = 1 - p with (n - k, k + 1) (binom_cumulative), so the binomial CDF's
failures are the beta's. Bank (baseline 70ecfa0b): BETA.DIST 971/2,822,
BETADIST 140/294, F.DIST 180/369, FDIST 653/4,399, TDIST 28/92, BINOM.DIST
70,320/70,349.

1. Mapping (fresh, 14,400 questions, fresh/BETA.DIST.map.json): a, b in
{0.3, 0.5, 0.9, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4.7, 7, 10, 20, 50, 100, 500}, 64 x per
pair (log-spaced near 0 and 1, uniform between).
- b = 1 (x^a): calc-func matches all. a = 1 (1 - (1 - x)^b): 693/896.
- Elsewhere exact agreement is 12-89% per pair, falling as a grows; the
  largest differences grow with a (about 10 ulp at a <= 3, 100-400 at a = 50,
  over 1,000 at a = 500). The differences are not confined to one region of
  x, so more than one of bratio's branches differs from Excel.
2. Error scale (a >= 20, 2,878 points, error against the true value in ulps
of the answer): Excel median 12.8, calc-func (TOMS 708) 15.6, a plain
lgamma prefactor exp(lgamma(a+b) - lgamma(a) - lgamma(b) + a ln x + b ln(1-x))
with the classic continued fraction 83.7. The correlation of Excel's errors
with TOMS 708's is 0.32, with the plain form 0.08.
Ranked candidates:
(a) TOMS 708 bratio with Excel's own leaves (its exp/log/gammaln and x87
    stores): consistent with the error scale; the branch-by-branch store
    order is not identified.
(b) Plain lgamma prefactor plus continued fraction: refuted by the error
    scale (six times Excel's).
(c) Closed forms at a = 1: 1 - pow(1 - x, b) 545/896, -expm1(b log1p(-x))
    573, 1 - exp(b ln(1 - x)) 574 (glibc leaves): all below calc-func's 693.
3. The a = 1 arm below x = 0.1 (identified and landed)
Dense map (fresh/BETA.DIST.a1.json, 3,000 calls, b in {0.3, 0.7, 1.5, 2, 3,
5.5, 10, 37, 100, 1000}, x log-uniform in [1e-9, 0.0999]): every difference
from calc-func sat below x = 0.1. With glibc leaves -expm1(b * log1p(-x))
already agrees within one ulp everywhere (62%, mean error 0.4-0.5 ulp in
every decade of x), so the shape was right and the leaves were not. Ranked
leaf programs on the same answers (research build, one switch):
  -expm1(b * log1p(-x)), Excel's leaves, binary64 product        1,767/3,000
  same with the product stored from x87                         1,767
  -expm1 of the x87 log1p_times chain (the leaf BINOM's x = 0
    mass already uses)                                          2,165
  internal or spilled EXPM1 graph of the binary64 product      2,364
  internal or spilled EXPM1 graph of the log1p_times chain     3,000
The spilled and internal graphs agree on 20,000 sampled (x, b) with x in
[1e-300, 0.1) and b in [1e-3, 1e6]: which one Excel runs is undetermined.
Fresh proof frozen before asking (q/BETA.a1-proof.json, 06:59 UTC):
BETA.DIST(x, 1, b) at random x in [1e-300, 0.1), b in [1e-3, 1e6]: 1,997 /
2,000 (previous program 430); the 100 doubles just below 0.1: 100/100 (72);
BETADIST: 298/300 (96). The five misses all have x in (1.1e-16, 2.3e-16),
one or two ulp: the log1p_times leaf's edge near 2^-53 is still open.

4. BINOM.DIST one short of n (identified and landed)
The same proof set asked BINOM.DIST(n - 1, n, p, TRUE) for 1 - p < 0.1:
409/500 matched the beta arm above, and Excel's own BETA.DIST(1 - p, 1, n)
(asked beside them) differs from Excel's BINOM.DIST on the other 91 by one or
two ulp. So the binomial has its own arm here. On those 500 answers the
binary64 product with the internal or spilled EXPM1 graph,
-expm1(n * log1p(-q)), matches 499. Fresh proof frozen before asking
(q/BINOM.n1-proof.json, 07:04 UTC): k = n - 1 with 1 - p in [1e-14, 0.1),
n up to 1e7: 998/1,000 (previous program 844); p in [0.5, 0.9]: 300/300
(unchanged arm); BINOM.DIST.RANGE(n, p, 0, n - 1): 200/200 (169); the 100
doubles on either side of p = 0.9: 100/100 (96).

6. Per-branch agreement on the shape map (calc-func before this round's
a = 1 arm; scripts/beta_branch.py replays bratio's branch choice):
  b = 1 arm 675/675, a = 1 arm 712/915 (now fixed below 0.1),
  a0, b0 > 1: bpser 2,366/3,938, bfrac 708/1,356, bup 1,059/2,448, basym 0/2;
  a0 or b0 <= 1: bpser 2,155/4,013, its mirror 148/396, bgrat 131/336,
  bup 94/321.
Every branch differs, so a shared stage is the likelier cause than one
branch's series. Storing bpser's prefactor exponent, its series steps, its
final product or its division by a from x87 (16 combinations, research
switch scripts/pb6.py, bpser_sw.py) changes none of the 7,951 bpser cells:
the difference is in the formula or its leaves, not in double rounding.
The prefactor alone (fresh/BETA.DIST.prefactor.json: 1,500 calls, a and b
in (1, 30), x in [1e-200, 1e-25], where the series is 1 to the last bit):
calc-func's exp(a ln x - betaln) / a matches 990, and so does the same with
Excel's own GAMMALN, LN and EXP leaves (any grouping of betaln, or the
exponent kept at x87); x^a * exp(-betaln) / a with Excel's POWER matches
1,081-1,098 by grouping; x^a * G(a+b) / (G(a) G(b)) / a with Excel's GAMMA
1,029-1,040. None is exact: the prefactor already differs where the series cannot, and
the power-based family is the closer one.
