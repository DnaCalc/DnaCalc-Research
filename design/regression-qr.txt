LINEST/LOGEST private QR: design note (round 42, Claude Opus, dna-firehorse)

Sources: Excel answers retained in evidence/round38 (qr-fresh, 216 designs x 6
selections per function) and round41 (regression-after-route, 15,150 LINEST
and 12,140 LOGEST selections), IEEE 754, published Householder QR with column
pivoting (Golub and Van Loan). No tier 3/4 implementation was read.

1. What the mapping shows

Of the 1,235 R41 LINEST selections still failing at c3646c36, 504 are degrees
of freedom (row 4, column 2) and 542 residual sums of squares (row 5, column 2),
almost all with a zero predictor column beside a collinear pair. Rank decisions
never depend on y, yet the failures do vary with column order:
- [0, x, 2x] without a constant: Excel fits 2x alone and leaves both x and the
  zero column out (df = n - 1, residual = fit on 2x over n - 1 rows).
- [0, 2x, x]: Excel keeps the zero column (it counts in df and consumes a row)
  when x's remainder after the 2x step is exactly zero (n = 4, 16); when it is
  a tiny positive number (n = 8, 32) the zero column is left out.
The current program removes a collinear column and carries on, so a zero
column reached later is always kept. That is the whole difference.

2. Program, ranked

(a) Rank-revealing stop (what a 1990s QR with column pivoting does): pivot on
    the largest remaining sum of squares; when the pivot fails the remainder
    test (running difference below 2^-41 times its initial sum of squares, or
    exactly zero), stop: every column not yet stepped is left out with it.
    Zero columns stepped before the stop still count, as before.
    R41 cohort: LINEST 13,915 -> 14,721 / 15,150 (+914, -108), LOGEST
    10,973 -> 11,781 / 12,140 (+916, -108). R38 designs, never used to find
    the rule: LINEST and LOGEST each 1,002/937 -> 1,233/1,168 / 1,296
    (+270, -39).
(b) Remove and continue (current): refuted by the cases above.
(c) The reflection sign of a diagonal entry that is exactly zero: the current
    alpha = -copysign(norm, x0) reflects +0 as a positive entry; Excel reflects
    it as a negative one (alpha = +norm), as `x0 > 0 ? -norm : norm` would. With
    a constant, x = {-3, -1, 1, 3}*2^k has a zero entry after the ones column's
    step, and Excel's answers for x equal the current program's for -x.
    On top of (a): LINEST 14,721 -> 14,864 (+143, -0), LOGEST 11,781 -> 11,861
    (+80, -0), R38 LINEST +6. Bank TREND 10 -> 12 / 12 (the only bank change).
Together (a) and (c) against c3646c36: R41 LINEST 13,915 -> 14,864 / 15,150
(+1,057, -108), LOGEST 10,973 -> 11,861 / 12,140 (+996, -108); R38 LINEST
1,002 -> 1,239 / 1,296 (+276, -39), LOGEST 937 -> 1,168 (+270, -39). Bank
3,887,837 -> 3,887,839 / 3,910,773; corpus unchanged by these two (its 90
LINEST/LOGEST failures are 1-14 ulp QR arithmetic and LOGEST overflow cases).
Remaining: with a constant and an exactly collinear pair, Excel keeps a zero
column that sits after the pair in the original order and leaves out one that
sits before it ([x, 2x, 0] and [2x, x, 0] count 2 predictors, [0, x, 2x] and
[0, 2x, x] count 1, for n = 4..32, ascending or descending). Without a
constant only the current-order tie decides. An exact x87 emulation raced 48
then 128 variants (ones column first or last, pivoted or fixed, centred or
not, swap or rotate, first or last tie, fresh or downdated criterion, stop or
remove); the best explains 55 of the 64 layout classes, so the rule is still
unidentified. All 147 losses (108 R41 + 39 R38) have a zero column beside a
collinear pair: 123 with a constant, 24 without (exact-tie cases at n = 8-32).

5. Round 43 (Claude Opus, dna-firehorse): the 41 open selections resolved

Order of the matrix. With a constant Excel stores the ones column after the
predictors and swaps it to the front: [x1, x2, x3, 1] -> [1, x2, x3, x1].
Pivoting is then plain swapping with the earlier column winning an exact
tie, with and without a constant. This one rule explains three things at
once: R42's "the later column wins a tie with a constant" (x1 now sits last),
the order-dependence of zero columns beside a collinear pair ([x, 2x, 0] and
[x, 0, 2x] keep the zero column, [0, x, 2x], [0, 2x, x] and [3x, 0, x] do
not), and F71/F99 ([0, x, -x]: x is fitted, not -x). The pivot's running
remainder is read as zero when rounding makes it negative (a collinear
column's remainder is often -2e-13); without the clip the zero column wins
those comparisons. Found with an x87 emulation of the R42 program
(~/codex-runs/r43/qr2.py, longdouble arithmetic) on the degrees of freedom
of 2,807 retained selections: current program 2,653, ones-last swap with the
clip 2,780; the remaining 27 were all n = 4 with a zero column and a pair
2^-42 apart.

The test while columns remain. Threshold maps (asked 06:05-06:08 UTC,
LINEST thr, thr2, thr3: 1,071 + 1,176 + 3,240 df questions, x fixed, the
perturbation stepped by 1/32 octave of the remainder ratio):
- [x, z] (z = 2x + d e_n), n = 4..32, with and without a constant: the switch
  is the remainder ratio 2^-41 (drop <= -41.000, keep >= -40.969).
- [x, z, w] with an independent w at any scale 2^-20..2^20: also 2^-41 once
  the ratio is taken after projecting out w (the earlier apparent shift was
  that projection).
- [x, z, 0], [0, x, z], [x, z, 0, 0], or a third column scaled 2^-400 or
  2^-600 (nonzero, independent, tiny): the switch moves to 2^-42.0 .. 2^-42.8,
  depending on x. In every one of these 20 configurations the switch is at
  vv = 2^-41 times x's initial sum of squares (drop <= -41.0008, keep >=
  -40.9978), where vv = rest + (x0 - alpha)^2 is the reflector's normaliser.
So: the last column is tested on its running remainder; a column with
columns still to come is tested on vv. Both against 2^-41 of the initial
sum of squares.

Landed (DnaCalc, regression_forecast_family.rs): ones column stored last and
swapped in; pivot remainder clipped at zero, earlier column wins ties; vv
test while columns remain. Retained cohorts (racer, invoke-inputs; error
names normalised, #NUM! = Num): R41 LINEST 14,852 -> 15,122 / 15,150, LOGEST
11,849 -> 12,119 / 12,140, R38 1,266 -> 1,296 and 1,267 -> 1,296 / 1,296,
R42 fresh sets 1-3 (both functions) +192; every cohort +791 / -0 in all.
Fresh proof (frozen 06:14 UTC, q/proof.sha256; 2,439 selections per
function in five families: G1 zero or tiny third column beside an exact or
perturbed pair, G2 permuted-entry ties, G3 pair near the switch beside an
independent column, G4 two zero/tiny columns, G5 full rank): LINEST 2,225 ->
2,413, LOGEST 2,255 -> 2,414. 25 selections per function (three designs with

Still open: last-bit standard errors of the intercept ([2,3], 21 R41
