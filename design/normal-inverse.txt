NORM.S.INV: design note (round 42, Claude Opus, dna-firehorse)

Sources: Excel answers retained in evidence/round39-41 (the R41 dense sweep:
128 windows of 8,192 consecutive doubles, 995,328 numeric answers), IEEE 754,
Wichura AS241 published tables. No tier 3/4 implementation was read.

1. What the mapping shows (before any program)

Regions, by the shape of the error against the true quantile (mpmath):
- p - 0.5 < -0.425 (p < 0.075): smooth sawtooth error, +1 ulp bias, the same
  answer for runs of adjacent p. It depends on p itself: pairs of p that share
  the stored q = p - 0.5 give different answers in every tail window.
- |q| <= 0.425: a function of the stored q only. No pair sharing a stored q
  differs (23,550 pairs in windows near 0.25, 0.2, 0.125, 0.1, 0.076). Odd: the
  windows just below and above 0.5 are exact negatives of each other.
- 0.925 < p <= 15/16: a third shape. Error sd 0.9 ulp, bias +0.8; Excel minus
  the AS241 tail is -1..+5 ulp (mean +2). One Newton step on Excel's NORM.S.DIST
  from AS241 does not reproduce it either.
- p > 15/16: the AS241 tail through 1 - p (R39), 1,283/1,283 retained answers.
The split at 0.925 is exact (window 72 changes at index 4096, p = 0.925), and
the split at 0.075 follows AS241's own test on the stored q.

Central noise. Within one window the error is Gaussian-like and uncorrelated
between neighbours, with sd growing steeply with |q|: 0.45 ulp near 0.5, 0.6 at
|q| = 0.2, 1.6 at 0.25, 3.9 at 0.30, 10 at 0.337, then 1.0 at 0.360, rising
again to 4.3 at 0.424 (both sides alike). Relative bias is +1.1e-16 out to
|q| = 0.25, then falls to -3.8e-16 at 0.337; after the break it restarts at
+1.5e-16 and falls to -2.6e-16 at 0.424. Excel's own NORM.S.DIST has 0.35 ulp
error there, so no root search on it explains the noise (tested: Newton/root
position, and two Newton steps on Excel's ERF give sd <= 1.1).
AS241's central rational expanded exactly into powers of q^2 and evaluated in
binary64 reproduces the noise sd within about 15% up to |q| = 0.337 (0.46,
0.49, 0.61, 1.8, 4.4, 5.0, 8.5 against Excel's 0.45, 0.48, 0.60, 1.55, 3.9,
5.8, 9.8), while its published r = 0.180625 - q^2 form stays near 0.7: the
noise is that of a monomial rational in q^2 with large alternating
coefficients. Its bias does not match, so the coefficients are not that
expansion rounded.

Near 0.5 (windows 64 and 65, 16,383 answers) one program is exact:
  t = 2q (exact);  y = RN53(RN64(t * K)),  K = RN(sqrt(pi)/2) = 0x3fec5bf891b4ef6b
  x = RN53(RN64(y * RN(sqrt 2)))
with zero mismatches; a single real constant (RN(q*C)) fails 26%, and the
AS241 near-half constants fail at least 35%. So the central branch is
NORM.S.INV(p) = sqrt(2) * erfinv(2p - 1), on the x87 at precision 64 with each
result stored, and the erfinv approximation leads with t*RN(sqrt(pi)/2).
The break between |q| = 0.337 and 0.360 is then |t| between 0.673 and 0.720:
the classic erfinv split at |t| = 0.7 (q = 0.35) lies inside it.

2. Candidate programs, ranked by what someone would have typed

(a) Lower tail: AS241 PPND16 tail at p, Horner products, sums and quotient each
    stored from precision 64, CRT log, binary64 sqrt. Candidate (not landed: no
    fresh answers this round): the R41 dense sweep goes 81,987 -> 393,140 /
    393,216 (+311,160, -7) and the R39/R40 retained answers 1,646 -> 2,715 /
    2,716 (upper > 15/16 included, no loss). Corpus +3 (LOGINV x2, LOGNORM.INV),
    bank unchanged (it holds no inverse-normal rows).
    76 tail answers remain (25 in the window near 2.2e-14, r about 5.6; the
    rest one or two per window); undetermined, not log precision alone
    (correctly rounded and extended log/sqrt variants are worse).
(b) Central: sqrt2 * erfinv(t), erfinv = t*P(t^2)/Q(t^2) (or t*(K + t^2*R)) in
    monomial Horner, two pieces split near |t| = 0.7, x87 stores. Degree and
    coefficients unknown; the bias curve per piece (error growing towards the
    far end of each piece, no equioscillation) suggests expansions about the
    piece start rather than minimax fits.
(c) 0.925 < p <= 15/16: unknown; neither AS241 tail nor Newton-refined AS241.
    Possibly erfinv's own outer piece (|t| > 0.85); its lower mirror is not
    used (p < 0.075 is the AS241 tail).
Refuted this round: root search on NORM.S.DIST; one Newton step from AS241;
two Newton steps on Excel's ERF (noise sd <= 1.1); a single constant near 0.5;
t / RN(2/sqrt(pi)) near 0.5 (2,632 of 16,383 fail); AS241 r-form; Pade
approximants [m/n] of erfinv(t)/t in t^2 with m + n <= 25, binary64 or x87-stored
(best 25% and 16% exact);
for (0.925, 15/16], AS241 tail coefficients scaled by 1/sqrt(2) then times
sqrt(2) (at most 12% exact).
