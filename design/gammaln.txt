9,408 inputs, old 8,883 exact; the ten-term stored form 9,408. No coefficient
is fitted. The ten terms already serve GAMMA's resident correction.

Plateau tool: both adjacent binary64 loads of 1/12 separate on the designed

Round 45 (GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex), same machine and source role)

New mapping: 25,187 arguments, including 24 consecutive 1,024-double
windows, roots, the .7/1.5/2/3/8 joins and small-argument exponent edges.
Both worksheet names agree on all 25,187 numeric answers. Independently
measured GAMMALN(1+x) minus LN(x) matches all 5,769 sampled x below .7.
The three declared shift-to-8 programs (one stored product then LN,
reverse subtraction of public logs, or sum of logs) all contradict the
lower windows, especially near the roots. The tested reflection composition
uses host SIN(pi*x) and host ln(pi), then measured LN and GAMMALN leaves;
it contradicts these windows but does not exclude other reflection leaves.
The first-order small-x series also contradicts ordinary lower-band
windows; agreement at sufficiently tiny x is not evidence of its use.

The existing Cody tables remain contradicted after exact algebraic basis
changes: x or 1/x, normalized or original coefficients, four fixed store
patterns. B2's factored formula becomes a degree-eight rational in x by
algebra alone. All 32 band/program combinations fail. The existing cleared
g=7 nine-coefficient Lanczos form also fails in all 12 sampled band/store/
precision combinations. This excludes those concrete candidates, not every
Lanczos form; no Numerical Recipes source was read or copied.

No coefficient changed and no degree was enlarged. The table frequencies
still require an independent hidden-stage reconstruction, so this item
stopped yielding before a replacement table could be justified. A research
DFT bug exposed by exact-root windows is fixed: a window touching or
crossing zero reports that condition instead of dividing by ulp(0).
Evidence: evidence/round45, gamma*, original audited cache and probe.py.

R49 / Fable-1 follow-up (GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex), dna-firehorse)

The ascending stored-product reduction and literal .7 join were already in
the application. Fresh public-leaf controls confirm 1,348/1,348 inputs in
(3,8), including single-bit windows around integers; x=3 is an exception:
GAMMALN(3)=3fe62e42fefa39f0, whereas LN(2)=3fe62e42fefa39ef and GAMMALN(2)=0.
The existing endpoint treatment is preserved. All 274 sampled x<.7 agree
with the public upper answer minus public LN. Neither final plain addition
nor PC64-then-store is separated by these controls. No new reduction or
lower-core implementation is credited to this round.

Fable's flat absolute-error premise is refuted. Its e1c records explicitly
say "math.lgamma (double)": the fallback, rather than the intended mpmath
reference, supplied its truth. Recomputing all 600 retained answers at 80
decimal digits gives max errors 7.32e-17,6.32e-17,6.30e-17,1.29e-16,3.16e-16
on [.7,1),[1,1.5),[1.5,2),[2,2.5),[2.5,3), respectively. The fallback's own
error reaches 1.12e-15. Its apparent noise cannot identify Excel's program.
The original answers and original fallback reference remain immutable.

Fresh mapping: 55 windows of 256 adjacent inputs, 14,080 numeric answers,
with high-precision loggamma of the exact binary64 inputs. Absolute error
shrinks at both roots: sd is about 1.23e-30 at 1 and 2.14e-30 at 2 in the
256-point windows. This supports factored roots, contradicting the proposed
difference of large Lanczos terms as the explanation for flat error.
For g=5,7,607/128,9, six predicted temporary binade crossings were probed on
both sides. The adjacent sd ratios lie between .914 and 1.044, not a doubling.
This refutes that signature for those temporaries; it excludes neither all
Lanczos forms nor every store policy. No g is recovered or coefficient fitted.
Typed decimal constants must still be rounded to PC64 before any binary64
store in a supported source-level program; this round identifies no such
new constant, and does not select arbitrary store masks.

Moved on because the proposed identifying signature depends on the faulty
reference, and its fresh crossing predictions fail. The lower rational
tables remain open. Full-bank beta/BINOM/NEGBINOM and corpus consumers were
rechecked before and after, with no numerical source change or gain.
Evidence: evidence/round49, gamma*, bank reports and corpus function counts.
