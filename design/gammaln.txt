GAMMALN: design note (round 44, GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex), dna-firehorse)

Sources: original question-service replies, Excel 16.0.20527; IEEE754;
the existing cleared GAMMALN and Stirling helpers; Bernoulli mathematics;
Cody/Stoltz SPECFUN algama (https://netlib.org/specfun/algama). Implementer;
no tier 3/4 implementation source or excluded checkout was read.

Mapping first: 18,314 arguments include consecutive 512-double windows at
integer/half-integer arguments, both roots, .7, 1.5, 3 and 8, and exponent
edges from the least normal number to 2^1000. GAMMALN and GAMMALN.PRECISE
agree on all 18,313 numeric answers; one unread remains a failure. No
Lanczos form is identified by this map. The
existing recurrence below 8 still depends on the unresolved lower tables.
R33's eight unexplained spectral peaks at 1.3 and seven at 1.625 already
refute the published tables' stage frequencies. Adjusting a few low bits
of those stand-ins cannot repair a different stage map; no lower table
was changed. The approximate B3 table extrapolated below 2 is also refuted.

Above 8: the main expression remains stored ((x-.5)*LN(x) - x) + ln(2pi)/2.
The correction is the existing ten Bernoulli terms in z^2, z=1/x, followed
by multiplication by z. Every operation stores from x87 PC64. Mapping the
places where the correction crosses half an output grid step separates
this from the previous fdlibm correction: 192 fixed crossing windows,
9,408 inputs, old 8,883 exact; the ten-term stored form 9,408. No coefficient
is fitted. The ten terms already serve GAMMA's resident correction.

First fresh frozen set: 10,212 inputs, old 9,881, plain binary64 ten-term
10,210. The two misses separate ordinary reciprocal division from its PC64
store: the reciprocal double rounding changes its last bit.
On this now-discovery set, nine terms have 15 contradictions, eleven one,
ten stored terms none. Second frozen set (new seed, predictions frozen
before requesting GAMMALN; 9,312 new arguments and 900 retained boundary
controls): old 9,875 -> 10,212/10,212. The new arguments alone improve
8,980 -> 9,312/9,312. The actual registered
application route also matches all 10,212. Larger arguments through 2^1000
and the transition at 8 are included; no assertion of a complete domain proof.

Another 2,048 new random arguments in [8,128], frozen through the existing
gamma tool and CachedService (including original socket frames, environment
definitions and exact input echoes), match 2,048/2,048. This corroborates the
earlier direct-client proofs, whose submitted requests and worker metadata
are retained; input echoes and socket frames were not separately recorded
for those early requests (the echo slots are null). The decisive earlier
controls are separately repeated with the audited client.

Plateau tool: both adjacent binary64 loads of 1/12 separate on the designed
grid; all 24 selected service questions agree with 1/12. No neighbour of
any other coefficient, or the ln(2pi)/2 load, separates on that finite grid:
their literal last bits remain undetermined. Reciprocal-square and
square-reciprocal coordinates have not been separated either. The landed
form uses the already present Bernoulli table and reciprocal-square tree.
Individual Horner store sites are not all uniquely determined; this is the
supported stored program, not a unique recovery of every instruction.

Open: rationals on [.7,1.5), [1.5,2), [2,3], propagated below .7 and through
the recurrence to 8; the GAMMA seed and reflection leaves. Full-function
bit exactness is not claimed. Evidence: evidence/round44.

Round 45 (GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex), same machine and source role)

New mapping: 25,187 arguments, including 24 consecutive 1,024-double
windows, roots, the .7/1.5/2/3/8 joins and small-argument exponent edges.
Both worksheet names agree on all 25,187 numeric answers. Independently
measured GAMMALN(1+x) minus LN(x) matches all 5,769 sampled x below .7.
The three declared shift-to-8 programs (one stored product then LN,
reverse subtraction of public logs, or sum of logs) all contradict the
lower windows, especially near the roots. The tested reflection composition
uses host SIN(pi*x) and host ln(pi), then measured LN and GAMMALN leaves;
it contradicts these windows but does not exclude other reflection leaves.
The first-order small-x series also contradicts ordinary lower-band
windows; agreement at sufficiently tiny x is not evidence of its use.

The existing Cody tables remain contradicted after exact algebraic basis
changes: x or 1/x, normalized or original coefficients, four fixed store
patterns. B2's factored formula becomes a degree-eight rational in x by
algebra alone. All 32 band/program combinations fail. The existing cleared
g=7 nine-coefficient Lanczos form also fails in all 12 sampled band/store/
precision combinations. This excludes those concrete candidates, not every
Lanczos form; no Numerical Recipes source was read or copied.

No coefficient changed and no degree was enlarged. The table frequencies
still require an independent hidden-stage reconstruction, so this item
stopped yielding before a replacement table could be justified. A research
DFT bug exposed by exact-root windows is fixed: a window touching or
crossing zero reports that condition instead of dividing by ulp(0).
Evidence: evidence/round45, gamma*, original audited cache and probe.py.
