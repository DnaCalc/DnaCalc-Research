YIELD stopping: design note (round 44, GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex), dna-firehorse)

Sources: original Excel 16.0.20527 question-service answers; elementary
zero-coupon bond mathematics and exact powers of two. Implementer;
no tier 3/4 implementation source or excluded checkout was read.

R35 and R41 already refuted RATE-style secants, bracket variants and fixed
iteration labels. A new separating design scales both price and redemption
by the same exact power of two, with zero coupons and settlement on a
coupon date. Then every mathematical root is unchanged, and a conventional
price calculation scales exactly. A relative-residual or yield-step stop
with scale-independent seeds would preserve the answer bits.

45 designs: frequencies 1/2/4, maturities 2/4/8 years, discount bases
1.001/1.05/1.25/1.5/2; 17 joint scales 2^-400 through 2^400, 765 questions
(758 numeric, 7 errors; every error remains in the denominator).
Every design changes answer under scaling. Some tiny-price cases return
#NUM or a spurious negative root; those remain failures, not numeric passes.
For n=2, frequency=1, price=25, redemption=100, the true root is exactly 1.
At scale 2^-400 Excel returns exactly 1; at scale 1 it is 2.69e-11 below;
at scales 2^100,2^200,2^400 it is 5.97e-11 below. Large cash scale plainly
does not force an absolute price residual below 1e-10. Thus neither a
plain absolute-price stop nor the scale-invariant complete solver above
explains the map. Initialisation, bracketing, or a mixed stopping test
depends on cash magnitude; a yield-width stop can still explain the
one-sided roughly 1e-10 errors.
No tolerance or seed is identified and no solver change is justified.
Next useful design: map the scale transitions with a fixed exact dyadic
root, then separate seed/bracket changes before fitting a stopping constant.
Evidence: evidence/round44, yield-scale.*; no ETS search was extended.

R46 continuation (same role and source restrictions)

Mapped5 zero-coupon designs over every cash exponent-500..500:5,005 calls.
Four designs use exact dyadic roots: n2/4, frequency1/2, discount base2;
the fifth uses base1.5 with binary64 price, so its root is only a control.
Scale changes have small- and large-cash limiting answers and a transition
around ordinary face amounts. At n2/frequency1, exact root1, Excel returns
1 at exponent-500,3feffffffffc4e9a at0,3feffffffff7cc5c at500.
This refines R44's scale map; it does not identify an iteration count.

Fixed cash-dependent guesses use face100, requested price/redemption,
average face/cash, capital gain per period, and fixed0/.1/.5/1 controls.
100 Newton/secant programs and150 expanding-bracket programs were tested
for necessary iterate membership on12 exponent-separated dyadic controls.
The brackets use bisection, alternating bisection/secant, regula falsi or
Illinois, with three simple expansion rules. Every program fails even a
16-ULP necessary-membership check; every ordinary verdict stays bit-exact.
This rejects those complete seed/iteration hypotheses, not all mixed tests
or all brackets. Analytic price evaluation and its stores are conditional;
the16-ULP padding is not a bound for another private price implementation.
No tolerance or cash-dependent seed was fitted. Moved on
because no complete tree survived to stopping-constant recovery.

Fallback:516 Poisson answers in adjacent windows around all4 larger corpus
differences equal CHISQ.DIST.RT(2*mean,2*(k+1)) exactly. The shared gamma tail
remains responsible; no second Poisson CDF is justified.774 binomial window
answers contradict the stored-complement public-beta relation221 times;
every contradiction has an inexact1-p. All520 exact-complement controls
agree (33 of254 inexact-complement controls agree too). Preserving both p
and binary64(1-p) in the existing bratio interface
still predicts only6/774 exactly; the beta body remains unresolved. No
distribution kernel changed. Evidence: evidence/round46.

R49 / Fable-1 backward plateau experiment (GPT-6.1 Sol (Codex))

Six zero-coupon designs, n=2/4/8 remaining periods, frequencies1/2, cover
every cash exponent -500..500: 6,006 numeric YIELD answers. The two-period
prices25/50/80 separate the small-cash root choices. In particular price80
again reaches the negative root. This excludes a solver constrained to the
positive discount-base basin; root choice alone does not prove Newton rather
than another unbounded iteration.

For analytic Newton in yield, z=1+y/f and r=price/redemption give
z_next=((n+1)z-r*z^(n+1))/n. Its inverse is cubic for n=2, not quadratic.
There are up to three real preimages, at most two positive. A quadratic inverse
would instead describe Newton on the discount factor, a different algorithm.
The corrected inverse keeps all three monotone branches, bisection brackets
at endpoints, n=2/4/8, and up to24 backward steps. Independent forward-map
controls check branch completeness, including z=2 and z=-4 both mapping to2
at n=2,r=1/4. The original incorrect negative-branch endpoint trial is retained
as superseded; only v3 supports these conclusions.

Inversion is conditional on the analytic slope. A 64-output-ULP envelope
tests sensitivity, not a rigorous bound for Excel's unknown price/slope.
No common proposed limit (2f/n,f/n,0,.1,-2f/n, or the declared cash limit)
survives all designs. For n=2,price25 the exact-root plateau admits broad
seed ranges; for price50, the 2f/n seed is excluded even by the padded
analytic inverse. Exact analytic Newton from 2f/n takes eleven iterates for
the negative price80 case, so Fable's suggested eight-step ceiling is also
not justified. Seeds y0(0),y0(infinity) are not recovered.

Moved on because the slope assumption and inverse conditioning prevent a
unique seed inference; fitting a cash formula now would fit the wrong map.
No solver, tolerance or seed constant changed. The valid ODDFYIELD scan
and the failure of the exactly regular first-period control are in the odd
first yield note. Evidence: evidence/round49, yield-* and audited cache.
